    HOW TO PLAY
    
          
    FIGHT YOUR WAY LEFT AND RIGHT, COLLECT 3 MICS, THEN HEAD TO THE EXIT ON THE FAR RIGHT. MICS COME FROM ENEMY DROPS AND BREAKABLE OBJECTS — NO ENEMY SHOWS WHETHER IT CARRIES ONE, SO EVERY DROP IS A SURPRISE. GRAB A BURGER TO HEAL.
    
          CRATES, HYDRANTS, BENCHES AND CONTAINERS ARE OBSTACLES: JUMP OVER THEM OR LAND ON TOP. ENEMIES HIT HARD, SO JUMPING TO DODGE WORKS TOO. MOHAWK PUNKS KEEP THEIR DISTANCE AND FLING THEIR HAIR AT YOU — IT FLIES SLOW, SO JUMP OVER IT OR CROUCH UNDER IT, BECAUSE A DIRECT HIT KNOCKS YOU BACK SIX TIMES HARDER THAN A NORMAL HIT! AND WHENEVER YOU LAND THE FINISHING BLOW ON ANY ENEMY, THE BODY IS KNOCKED BACK WITH FIVE TIMES THE USUAL FORCE AND FADES AWAY AS IT SLIDES. ONLY ONE MOHAWK SHOWS UP AT A TIME, AND AFTER A HAIR THROW IT CANNOT THROW AGAIN FOR 8 SECONDS. A STAGE'S BOSS ONLY SHOWS HIMSELF ONCE YOU HAVE FOUND ALL 3 MICS — COLLECT THEM AND HE COMES TO YOU. THE MOMENT A BOSS APPEARS, EVERY GRUNT ON THE FIELD VANISHES AND NO NEW ONES SHOW UP — FROM THEN ON IT IS JUST YOU AND THE BOSS. BOSSES TURN INVINCIBLE FOR 1 SEC AFTER EVERY HIT AND LEAP AT YOU — STRIKE RIGHT AFTER THEY LAND! WHEN A BOSS APPEARS, THE MUSIC SWITCHES TO THAT BOSS'S OWN FASTER HIP HOP TRACK — BOSS MUSCLE AND BOSS COAT EACH HAVE A DIFFERENT ONE — UNTIL HE FALLS. RAPID RUSH PUNCHES THROUGH BOSS GUARD — WHILE THE RUSH IS FIRING, BOSS INVINCIBILITY DOES NOT WORK. THE FINAL BOSS, BOSS COAT, TAKES ONLY HALF DAMAGE, FLINGS HIS HAT FROM FAR AWAY (5 PER FIGHT — IT FLIES LOW, SO JUMP IT, CROUCHING WILL NOT SAVE YOU) AND SPINS HIS COAT UP CLOSE (3 PER FIGHT): GET CAUGHT AND YOU FLY ALL THE WAY TO THE EDGE OF THE STAGE! ANY ENEMY — BOSS INCLUDED — THAT CAMPS IN ONE SPOT FOR A WHILE WITHOUT HURTING YOU LEAPS AWAY TO A NEW POSITION. BEAT THE FINAL BOSS AND YOU CAN REGISTER YOUR SCORE AND CLEAR TIME IN THE ONLINE RANKING — IT IS PUBLIC, SO EVERY PLAYER CAN SEE THE TOP 8, AND THE VICTORY SCREEN PLAYS ITS OWN CELEBRATION HIP HOP GROOVE WHILE YOU DO! YOUR PLAY TIME RUNS AT THE TOP OF THE SCREEN (TIME) FROM THE MOMENT YOU PRESS GAME START UNTIL THE FINAL CLEAR.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    LEVEL UP & SPECIAL MOVES
    
          
    EACH STAGE SENDS A LIMITED CREW AT YOU — NO ENDLESS BACKUP. WAVES PLUS REINFORCEMENTS ADD UP TO ROUGHLY A LEVEL AND A HALF OF XP PER STAGE, AND LEVELING UP RAISES MAX HP, POWER AND SPEED, AND UNLOCKS NEW MOVES.
    
          EXTRA LIVES: EVERY 2 LEVELS YOU GAIN (LV 3, 5, 7...) EARNS ONE SPARE LIFE, SHOWN AS ×N NEXT TO YOUR FACE IN THE HUD. GET KNOCKED OUT WITH A LIFE IN STOCK AND YOU BURN IT TO REVIVE ON THE SPOT AT FULL HP, BLINKING AND UNTOUCHABLE FOR 2 SECONDS WHILE THE FIGHT GOES ON. GET KNOCKED OUT WITH NO LIVES LEFT AND IT IS GAME OVER — YOU START AGAIN FROM STAGE 1, BACK AT LV 1.
    
          EVERY SPECIAL BURNS THE GOLD GAUGE UNDER YOUR HP BAR — POSE INCLUDED. IT REFILLS ON ITS OWN OVER A FEW SECONDS, AND THE HIGHER A MOVE'S LEVEL, THE MORE GAUGE IT COSTS: POSE 25 / RUSH 35 / REC 45 / VINYL 60 / TRIO 100 (FULL GAUGE). ONCE ANY SPECIAL FIRES, ALL SPECIALS LOCK — THE BAR TURNS RED — UNTIL THE GAUGE REFILLS ALL THE WAY TO FULL.
    
          LV 2 — RAPID RUSH: ONE PRESS AND SIX ATTACKS FIRE ON THEIR OWN AT FULL SPEED — CROUCH ATTACK, PUNCH, KICK, THEN THE SAME THREE AGAIN. NO MASHING NEEDED — AND YOU CAN KEEP MOVING LEFT AND RIGHT WHILE IT FIRES.
    
          LV 3 — RECORD THROW: PRESS THE REC BUTTON TO FLING 3 RECORDS STRAIGHT AHEAD. EACH RECORD SHATTERS ON THE FIRST ENEMY IT HITS, AND SMASHES OBJECTS TOO. NO CHARGING ANYMORE — ONE PRESS AND THEY FLY.
    
          LV 4 — VINYL BURST: RECORDS BURST OUT AROUND YOU AND SHATTER CLOSE BY — ONLY ENEMIES NEAR YOU GET HIT. THINK OF IT AS A STRONGER, WIDER POSE.
    
          LV 5 — TRIO ATTACK: ALL THREE HEROES FIGHT TOGETHER — THE OTHER TWO APPEAR AT YOUR LEFT AND RIGHT AS LV15 FIGHTERS, WHATEVER YOUR OWN LEVEL IS: THEIR SPEED, PUNCH, KICK AND POSE ALL USE THE LV15 STAT CURVE FOR THEIR OWN HERO. THEY ARRIVE WITH ONLY 15% OF THAT LV15 HP, THEN ROAM THE WHOLE STAGE ON THEIR OWN HUNTING DOWN ENEMIES, AND STAY UNTIL ENEMIES BEAT THEM DOWN — THERE IS NO TIME LIMIT. IT COSTS THE WHOLE GAUGE, AND AFTERWARDS THE GAUGE REFILLS SLOWLY UNTIL IT IS FULL AGAIN — THEN IT GOES BACK TO NORMAL SPEED.

    TOUCH CONTROLS
    
          
    SLIDE THE STICK LEFT / RIGHT TO MOVE, SLIDE IT UP TO JUMP AND SLIDE IT STRAIGHT DOWN TO CROUCH (DIAGONAL-DOWN JUST MOVES), AND HIT THE BIG WORN ARCADE BUTTONS TO FIGHT. THE SPECIAL BUTTONS SIT IN THE TOP ROW AND POSE / KICK / PUNCH BELOW. EVERY SPECIAL — POSE INCLUDED — BURNS THE GOLD GAUGE UNDER YOUR HP BAR, AND THE NUMBER ON EACH BUTTON IS ITS COST. THE RUSH, REC, VINYL AND TRIO BUTTONS LIGHT UP AS YOU UNLOCK THEM. WHILE CROUCHED, PUNCH AND KICK THROW LOW CROUCH ATTACKS.
